01 / THE MODEL

Not just a creator.
Every role is yours.

At PeepHoLez, eligible Broadcaster Members aren't locked into one identity. The same membership lets you host a live experience, appear in another creator's broadcast, or simply watch.

◉

Primary Broadcaster

Take the lead. Create and host your own live PeepHole and invite eligible guests.

✧

Guest Broadcaster

Join another Primary Broadcaster's experience and participate as a featured guest.

◌

Viewer

Enjoy another Broadcaster's live event at no additional live-viewing charge.

Today you're the Viewer. Tomorrow you're the Guest. Next week you're the Primary.

03 / THE OPPORTUNITY

More than one way to earn.

Live PeepHole Passes

Eligible Primary and Guest Broadcasters participate in earnings generated by paid live-event access, according to the event's payout rules.

100% Tipping

Tips are intended to go 100% to the tipped creator. Payment-processing treatment will be disclosed before launch.

The Library

Recorded broadcasts can create additional sales opportunities. A stored video costs $3—even if you watched its live event for free.

Creator Bonuses

Eligible broadcasts may qualify for additional rewards under the platform's bonus rules.

04 / A HIDDEN REVENUE BOOSTER

Meet MANIPULATION.

A distinct premium viewing channel designed to introduce additional paid demand to eligible live broadcasts—without changing the creator-first experience.

Creator control. The Primary Broadcaster determines whether eligible premium access is enabled before publishing an event.

Separate paid access. MANIPULATION viewers participate through a premium viewing model.

Boundaries built in. These viewers cannot broadcast or directly communicate with creators.
